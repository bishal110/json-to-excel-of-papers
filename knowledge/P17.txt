Knowledge record: P17-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Gradient-boosted tree ensembles using XGBoost, CatBoost and Light GBM tool families; source reports horizon models rather than a fully reproducible winner configuration.

Inputs: 1153 wells, 2070 failures 2014–2020; daily measurements; 30 variables consolidated 9; derived UNIFLOC hydraulic/power features, 85% separation assumption; time statistics and cumulative work.

Outputs: Failure probability at 1, 2, 3, 7, 14 days.

Reported performance: Preprocessed horizons 1/2/3/7/14 d: ROC-AUC .944/.889/.869/.735/.625; precision .879/.809/.785/.655/.601; recall .871/.809/.781/.726/.624; F1 .875/.809/.783/.689/.612. Raw 1 d AUC .870, F1 .770. Table 3–4 p.13 visually verified.

Advantages: Large fleet, held-out 20% wells(397 test failures), physical preprocessing improves results.

Limitations: Balanced 50/50 class evaluation; interpolated/imputed values are not recovered measurements. Akima≤5 day gaps and long-gap moving-average/linear fills may use future data unless made causal. Component/manufacturer/stage metadata unavailable.

Data feasibility: Needs equipment, trajectory, PVT, failure records and physical model beyond three sources. Surface-only version is a new experiment, not established by results.

Complexity: High

Prediction target: Aggregate failure risk, strongest near-term horizon.

Lead time qualification: 1–3 days supported more strongly than 7–14 days. This is forecast-label horizon, not demonstrated maintenance lead for every event.

Signals: Daily telemetry, production context, resistance, physics and exposure features.

Source thresholds: Probability .5 in balanced classification; not a fleet-calibrated operating alarm.85% separation is a modeling assumption.

Implementation proposal: Use strictly past-only filling; show missingness; split by run/well/time; recalibrate for rare actual failures and compare measured-only versus inferred-feature models.

Recommended future model: Short-horizon boosting with measured-only and physics-assisted ablations.

Review status: Pending

Source caveats: 96% post-fill completeness describes filled dataset, not sensor coverage. No direct water-injection diagnosis.
