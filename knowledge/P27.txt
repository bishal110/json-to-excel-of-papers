Knowledge record: P27-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: PCA to 3 dimensions plus least-squares enclosing sphere.

Inputs: 13 variables, 20 min samples, 174538 stable records from 47 wells; 40 normal comparison wells use daily report data.

Outputs: Stable versus anomalous/failure trajectory.

Reported performance: TP20, FN27, TN40, FP0; event recall 42.55%, calculated overall accuracy 68.97%, observed precision 100% in this small selected sample. Paper calls 42.5%'accuracy' but it is failure recall.3 PCs retain~70% variance.

Advantages: Simple interpretable healthy-boundary monitor; explicitly reports missed failures.

Limitations: Normal/failure sampling resolution differs; only 70% variance retained; 20 min samples miss fast dynamics; same wells contribute healthy/failure periods; small selected normal set.

Data feasibility: Needs 13 inputs including downhole signals and choke. Sensor-lost model requires new baseline and separate validation; daily Excel context does not recreate 20 min telemetry.

Complexity: Medium

Prediction target: Some developing faults; abrupt failures may only be detected.

Lead time qualification: Unquantified; 20 events before recorded failure.

Signals: 13 synchronized channels with mode/quality metadata.

Source thresholds: Maximum training distance, not physical safety limit.

Implementation proposal: Use high-frequency Avalon data and regime-specific baselines; compare PCA residual/SPE and T² with sphere; test independent runs and record false alerts per operating hour.

Recommended future model: PCA baseline with both retained-space and residual-space monitoring.
