Knowledge record: P32-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Per-ESP PCA T² versus XGBoost motor-vibration regression residual with SPC aggregation.

Inputs: Six failed ESPs>1 year history; initial windows grown 20,000 min to 210,000 min; preferred 180,000 min~125 days; sequential 80/20 within-window validation for regression.

Outputs: Abnormal minute/day and persistent failure advisory.

Reported performance: Table 1 PCA false alarms 1, 4, 0, 3, 5; leads 14, 7, 1, 1, 3 days (first 5). Nonlinear false alarms 1, 11, 12, 10, 6; leads 14, 41, 10,120, 13. Sixth poor-quality well: PCA19 false/2 days, nonlinear 2 false/327 days, excluded from averages. No classification accuracy or independent nonfailure cohort.

Advantages: Chronological per-well construction and explicit alarm persistence tradeoff.

Limitations: Six failed wells only; parameter selection retrospective; no fleet rate denominator; two-day persistence delays fast faults; real-power proxy I×V omits three-phase power factor/efficiency.

Data feasibility: PCA needs pressure/rate/temperature; residual method requires measured vibration at scoring, not just surface predictors. Cannot calculate residual after losing vibration sensor.

Complexity: Medium

Prediction target: Persistent abnormal operation or earlier recognition of existing failure.

Lead time qualification: Abstract~2 weeks; detailed means 5/40 days. Source explicitly says one event already catastrophic for 14 days before confirmation—do not label all lead as prevention time.

Signals: Synchronized minute telemetry, actual vibration for residual method, independent onset/confirmation dates.

Source thresholds: PCA nominal tail.0027; residual.01;>12/>195 minutes daily and 2 days persistence. These statistical assumptions are not valid fleet guarantees under autocorrelation.

Implementation proposal: Tune causal alarm persistence by fault urgency; preserve fast protective systems; count missing minutes separately; validate on healthy and failed runs.

Recommended future model: Vibration residual benchmark only on wells retaining reliable vibration.

Review status: Pending

Source caveats: Table 1 visually verified.180000 minutes is study-specific baseline length. Do not suppress an urgent physical alarm merely because it lasted only one day.
