Knowledge record: P11-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Attribute forward selection; linear/polynomial regression and neural-network life regression

Inputs: Primarily static equipment/geometry/production, sand proxy and operating-cycle duration

Outputs: Pump service life/MTBF-type estimate

Reported performance: ESP R²: linear 0.71, neural network 0.81; beam 0.10/0.12/0.29. R² is not classification accuracy. Authors do not recommend general deployment due to limited data.

Advantages: Integrates heterogeneous records; interpretable factor priorities.

Limitations: Post-pull HUD risks target leakage; censoring treatment and held-out validation unclear; correlated feature weights are not causes; only failure cases may bias life model.

Data feasibility: Three sources alone insufficient: need installations/pulls/failure labels, completion geometry, equipment and contemporaneous solids data. Downhole sensors not essential to original static model.

Complexity: Medium

Prediction target: Service-life estimate conditional on recorded attributes.

Lead time qualification: No early-warning horizon; post-pull predictor compromises prospective use.

Signals: Static metadata, operation cycles, production and valid pre-event solids metric.

Source thresholds: No ESP alarm thresholds. Beam geometry recommendations must remain separate.

Implementation proposal: Assemble run-level dataset including censored survivors; replace HUD leakage; validate survival/life models on later installations.

Recommended future model: Survival model and run-life regression with contemporaneous inputs; failure-report extraction.

Review status: Pending

Source caveats: Reported ESP shares ~49% cable, 30% pump, 12% other and 7% motor are approximate and do not sum exactly to 100%. Do not force normalization or treat percentages as universal.
