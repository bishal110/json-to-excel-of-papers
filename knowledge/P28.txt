Knowledge record: P28-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Standardized PCA2 D plus Azure two-class boosted decision tree screenshot.

Inputs: 8 variables abstract versus 10 Dmethod; 3 failed+1 operating case; tree 70/30 split.

Outputs: Normal/failed status and PCA drift.

Reported performance: PCA PC1=78.47%, PC2=9.94%, total 88.41% variance, not accuracy. Screenshot positive class NORMAL: TP441, FN0, FP14, TN56; accuracy.973, precision.969, recall 1, F1.984, AUC.927. Recast failure-positive: TP56, FN14, FP0, TN441 =>failure recall 80%, precision 100% in reported sample.

Advantages: Direct sensor-loss/ground-fault examples and frequency-conditioning lesson.

Limitations: Small cases; automatic real-time deployment future work; tree labels/sample independence unspecified; months-long retrospective drift can reflect onset/discovery ambiguity.

Data feasibility: Full PCA unavailable after downhole loss; build separate surface baseline. Electrical integrity/DIFA/metadata are additional required records.

Complexity: Medium

Prediction target: Deteriorating multivariate behavior, especially electrical cases; no validated RUL.

Lead time qualification: Well A drift April 2018, failure June 2018(~2 months), source says 3 months; Well B failure-region May 2016 precedes March 2017 sensor issue 10 months and August 2017 terminal failure; not transferable forecast horizon.

Signals: Frequency-specific telemetry and quality/electrical integrity records.

Source thresholds: Tree.5; source typical undervoltage 20% off nameplate requires actual OEM/site settings. No universal PCA radius.

Implementation proposal: Keep sensor loss separate from physical fault; retain mode-specific baseline; independently test surface-only model and alert-to-confirmed-fault timing.

Recommended future model: Sensor-availability-aware anomaly models and electrical degradation risk.

Review status: Pending
