Knowledge record: P36-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Generic multilayer neural-network anomaly detector; architecture/loss not disclosed.

Inputs: 30 onshore ESPs, 200–500 k W; equipment variants grouped; one-year normal training baseline; surface/downhole telemetry.

Outputs: Anomaly level and prioritized advisory.

Reported performance: 12 day retrospective example; no precision, recall, confusion matrix, false alerts per well-month or independent event denominator.

Advantages: Learns complex normal relationships; can highlight previously unrecognized anomalies.

Limitations: Manual training-data curation; repeated tuning on same history; missing cause specificity; limited reproducibility; related publications reuse pilot evidence.

Data feasibility: Potential Avalon-fed monitoring, but full published model uses downhole inputs. A sensor-lost cohort requires new baseline and validation; Excel tests supply contextual evidence rather than fast stream.

Complexity: High

Prediction target: Abnormal behavior preceding some interruptions; not RUL or unique cause.

Lead time qualification: One 12 day case; no guaranteed horizon.

Signals: Seven process variables plus quality/event records.

Source thresholds: ~80% versus<40% anomaly-score examples; final operating cutoff undisclosed.

Implementation proposal: Pilot in advisory mode with cohort-specific baseline and prospective event/alert audit; compare simple PCA/tree baselines before deep model.

Recommended future model: Normal-behavior multivariate anomaly model per sensor/equipment cohort.

Review status: Pending

Source caveats: Overlaps P04/P06/P08/P36/P37 pilot family; do not count repeated 12 day example as independent validation. Conventional alarms can also be preventive; broad vendor contrast is not engineering fact. Source typography 2000 C likely intended 200°C; not a valid equipment operating limit.
