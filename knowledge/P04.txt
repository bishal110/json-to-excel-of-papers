Knowledge record: P04-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Generic multilayer neural networks, Python/TensorFlow; one model per ESP variant; cloud anomaly monitoring

Inputs: Annulus and production pressure, current, speed, intake pressure, intake temperature, motor temperature

Outputs: Anomaly indication described probabilistically; human selects mitigation

Reported performance: No accuracy, recall, precision or false-alarm rate reported. One 12-day retrospective warning; qualitative pilot success.

Advantages: Learns normal relationships and can flag unfamiliar patterns; independent SCADA validation.

Limitations: Architecture/training details undisclosed; causality unclear; manual baseline cleansing; unknown anomalies hard to interpret; repeated historical tuning risks optimism.

Data feasibility: Avalon plus surface pressures can supply some inputs if tags exist; published model still needs three downhole channels. Production tests alone are insufficient. Reduced-sensor model requires separate training/validation.

Complexity: High

Prediction target: Abnormal operation potentially preceding failure; not a verified failure mode or exact date.

Lead time qualification: 12 days in one historical case; hours-to-days threshold tradeoff.

Signals: Seven synchronized inputs, variant and historical events.

Source thresholds: Source examples >80% and <40%; not proven probabilities or field alarm settings.

Implementation proposal: Train on adjudicated healthy regimes, hold out complete wells/runs, score in shadow mode, calibrate false alerts and provide evidence to engineers.

Recommended future model: Normal-behavior anomaly detector with separate sensor-available and surface-only versions.

Review status: Pending

Source caveats: Likely overlaps the 30-ESP/12-day use case in the cited SPE-192610-MS paper; do not count repeated descriptions as independent evidence.
