Knowledge record: P24-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: PCA+XGBoost; hourly median, 1.5 IQR filter, standardization, moving differences.

Inputs: 339089 normal, 1728 preworkover, 288 workover hourly points; 8 physical signals+differences.

Outputs: Normal versus≤7 day pre-event; discussion uses normal/preworkover/workover classes.

Reported performance: 10 fold mean ROC-AUC.95; test pre-event precision.80, recall.60, F1.71, support 604; normal 101726. Abstract says>0.71, table.71.

Advantages: Clear physical polymer mechanism and explicit imbalanced-class metrics.

Limitations: 70/30 versus 70/15/15 split descriptions; random folds not well/run chronological validation; full-data PCA/scaling language risks leakage; tables contain impossible descriptive ordering(mean/std versus max); standardization text says variance rather than SD.

Data feasibility: Full model needs downhole sensors and workover labels. Three sources alone insufficient unless labels/equipment context supplied. Sensorless variant untested.

Complexity: Medium

Prediction target: Electrical preworkover state.

Lead time qualification: ≤7 day label; point recall 60% does not equal 60% event recall or guaranteed 7 day warning.

Signals: Eight synchronized signals, causal moving differences, independent failure/workover records.

Source thresholds: 1.5 IQR is cleaning threshold, not fault limit; no physical operating cutoff.

Implementation proposal: Preserve genuine extremes; fit transforms on training only; chronological ESP-run holdout; report event recall, alarm duration, lead and false alerts.

Recommended future model: PCA+boosting benchmark alongside raw-feature boosting, full sensor and surface-only cohorts.

Review status: Pending
