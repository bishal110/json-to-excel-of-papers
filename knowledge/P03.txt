Knowledge record: P03-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Spreadsheet fuzzy inference; triangular memberships; product AND, OR and Most-of coupling; rank DOF directly without separate defuzzification

Inputs: Eight daily-median trend variables; slopes normalized with arctangent

Outputs: Scores for nine failure-mode hypotheses and possible generic risk indication

Reported performance: No validated accuracy. System did not identify actual root causes in five failures. Many zero premises defeated product AND; Most-of remained low. Table 2 includes example scores, not probabilities.

Advantages: Transparent and spreadsheet-compatible; offshore dataset and explicit rule matrix.

Limitations: Nine selected wells; actual causes not established; scale/unit-sensitive arctangent; common memberships; trip contamination; three identical rule vectors; 30/60-day gaps.

Data feasibility: Avalon+production+surface-pressure data could support full rules only where all eight inputs exist. Lost PIP/PDP/temperature makes published rules incomplete; do not replace missing conditions by true.

Complexity: Medium

Prediction target: Possible developing distress/risk, not specific failure or RUL.

Lead time qualification: 7/30/60 days are analysis windows, not proven warning lead times.

Signals: Eight signals plus event masks and confirmed outcomes.

Source thresholds: Figure 3 prints D=(-100,-0.6,-0.2), C=(-0.5, 0, 0.5), I=(0.2, 0.6,100). Curve shoulders and text's triangle notation need careful implementation; no validated alert DOF threshold.

Implementation proposal: Implement as explainable hypothesis scores, with unit-consistent slopes, event segmentation and missing-signal coverage; validate specificity before operational use.

Recommended future model: Unit-aware fuzzy risk triage; learned membership functions; comparison with supervised failure labels.
