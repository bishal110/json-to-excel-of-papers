Knowledge record: P25-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Gaussian-kernel SVR with genetic-algorithm tuning of C, epsilon, kernel width; ten input combinations.

Inputs: J200 N test circuit, 1800/3000 rpm, inlet 100/200 bar, gas 0–3 kg/h; 80/20 train/test.

Outputs: Gas mass flow estimate.

Reported performance: Best total-flow+head model MSE .0027 (~.003), reported correlation .997. Vibration-only MSE .022, correlation .983; flow-only .930/.597. Units/scaling of MSE need source-data verification.

Advantages: Demonstrates value of combining hydraulic signals; vibration not required for best laboratory model.

Limitations: Narrow test domain; no offshore validation, failure outcomes or RUL; head/elevation is not simply well depth. Some estimates negative at zero gas.

Data feasibility: Not directly: continuous total flow and developed head at matched conditions required. Sparse surface tests and no downhole sensors do not reproduce these inputs; requires PVT/calibrated hydraulic model and gas-reference labels.

Complexity: Medium

Prediction target: Gas mass flow soft sensor, not future failure.

Lead time qualification: None established.

Signals: Total flow and developed head preferred; optional raw vibration/torque.

Source thresholds: Source introduction says gas-oil ratio should not exceed 10%; definition/dimensions ambiguous and pump-specific—do not use as universal gas fraction trip.

Implementation proposal: Calibrate against multiphase measurements and physical head calculation across local speed/fluid domain; reject extrapolation and negative unphysical outputs.

Recommended future model: Physics-assisted gas-flow/void-fraction estimator after obtaining suitable reference data.

Review status: Pending

Source caveats: Paper's broad claim that failures can be easily avoided by changing conditions is not demonstrated field control validation.
