Knowledge record: P31-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: BP neural network 45 inputs–15 hidden–10 outputs plus analytical pressure-buildup model.

Inputs: Field and synthetic current cards; abstract training across>900 wells; at least 50 simulated examples per class; 45 features from 24 h cards; hydraulic model needs fluid/completion/pump curve.

Outputs: Ten current-card classes plus hydraulic diagnosis.

Reported performance: No held-out aggregate accuracy. Case output 57% normal, 12.3% sand failed to identify leak; pressure method led to confirmed tubing leak.

Advantages: Directly addresses absence of downhole sensors using additional surface test evidence.

Limitations: Synthetic training and validation not fully characterized; current classes overlap; Figure 5 rendering partly corrupted; empirical 0.78/1.5/1 cutoffs not universal.

Data feasibility: Current features possible from Avalon; full pressure method needs dedicated time-resolved test and extra completion/PVT/curve data. Routine Excel pressures/tests alone insufficient.

Complexity: High

Prediction target: Present operational class and integrity/performance fault; no validated RUL.

Lead time qualification: No quantified advance warning; 24 h card is lookback.

Signals: Current plus engineered WHP response and physical model.

Source thresholds: Pressure ratio 0.78/1.5, current ratio 1; learning rate.4 is model setting, not field alarm.

Implementation proposal: Use production-current mismatch as screening; retain multiple causes; implement pressure interpretation only for approved acquired tests with uncertainty.
