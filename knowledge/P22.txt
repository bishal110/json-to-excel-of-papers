Knowledge record: P22-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Deterministic engineering algorithms and virtual performance indicators; not trained ML.

Inputs: Pump curves, density, PI, electrical/downhole/surface variables.

Outputs: Envelope, motor load, rate estimates and prioritized relax/restrict/rerating candidates.

Reported performance: No prediction accuracy or false-alarm statistics.4.6 year run life is operational reported outcome, not controlled effect.

Advantages: Transparent and practical; basic/medium/high instrumentation tiers.

Limitations: Rate/head inference requires density and calibration; claimed benefits lack control cohort.

Data feasibility: Basic electrical surveillance feasible if tags mapped. Full hydraulic envelope needs valid downhole pressures or independently validated estimates plus curves/PVT/completion data outside three sources.

Complexity: Medium

Prediction target: Off-envelope operation and impending trip risk; no RUL prediction.

Lead time qualification: Not quantified.

Signals: Telemetry plus curves/nameplates/fluid data.

Source thresholds: Local curve limits and nameplates; 60→80% loading is an example, not alarm threshold.

Implementation proposal: Start with electrical/rating dashboard, add pressure-based duty for instrumented wells, display uncertainty for sensor-lost wells.

Recommended future model: Physics-based virtual rate/efficiency; constrained optimization as later stage.
