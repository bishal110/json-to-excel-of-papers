Knowledge record: P15-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Simulink multiphysics twin; synthetic data generator; future hybrid AI module

Inputs: Detailed asset/fluid/reservoir/completion metadata and live telemetry

Outputs: Full signal simulation, latent residuals, training signatures; diagnosis/RUL are intended AI capabilities

Reported performance: No field predictive accuracy, residual threshold or RUL error. AI module and field prototype/trials are future work.

Advantages: Couples thermal/electrical/hydraulic behavior; represents transients; synthetic fault coverage.

Limitations: Calibration/identifiability, uncertain physics, sim-to-real gap; stochastic life assignment is not observed RUL; no deployed validation.

Data feasibility: Three user data sources alone insufficient without equipment, completion, fluid and reservoir model. Virtual sensing for missing downhole channels is an opportunity, not demonstrated accuracy here.

Complexity: High

Prediction target: Simulated response and synthetic fault signatures; future RUL/diagnostic capability.

Lead time qualification: Not reported.

Signals: System metadata, telemetry and calibration references.

Source thresholds: No operational alarm limits; two-minute simulation target is computational cadence.

Implementation proposal: Begin with calibrated hydraulic/electrical residuals and independent tests; add thermal model and missing-sensor estimates with uncertainty before full twin.

Recommended future model: Virtual sensor with uncertainty; hybrid residual classifier; synthetic-data augmentation tested on real withheld runs.

Review status: Pending

Source caveats: Autonomous-control/RUL capabilities are requirements, not demonstrated outcomes. No water-injection model validated.
