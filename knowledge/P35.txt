Knowledge record: P35-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Isolation Forest→PCA5 PCs→CatBoost/ExtraTrees with temporal features; SVM/MLP comparators; SMOTE/class weights reported.

Inputs: One selected well of 7,105 day window, 87390 raw records; 83020 final prose versus 82350 table; 30 s claimed sampling.

Outputs: Rule-derived three-state class.

Reported performance: Abstract accuracy 94.2%, failure recall 96.5%; Table 9 CatBoost 99.85% accuracy/F1~99.83%; Table 10/11 CV97.2% accuracy, F1~97.2%; ExtraTrees 96.8% test/93.8% CV. These are distinct conflicting reports, not one validated score.

Advantages: Useful past-window trend features and comparison of practical tabular models.

Limitations: One well, selected anomalous window; labels depend on same signals as features; no independent event-level prospective validation; chronological versus stratified K-fold descriptions conflict; preprocessing/SMOTE placement unclear; outlier removal may discard true faults; claimed 5 PC variance 90/95/97% inconsistent.

Data feasibility: Requires working downhole pressure/temperature/vibration; surface voltage/current prior to transformer must not be treated as motor-terminal values. Missing-sensor version untested.

Complexity: Medium

Prediction target: Operational state according to retrospective rules; superiority in future failure prediction not established.

Lead time qualification: No validated forecast horizon; 2–4 h/1 h conditions and 4 h plots are not demonstrated advance warning.

Signals: Eight physical variables plus causal engineered trends and independent outcomes.

Source thresholds: 90°C,.02 g delta,−100 psi/min are study-specific and internally inconsistent with distribution; not approved ESP limits.

Implementation proposal: Benchmark direct rules against trees using independent DIFA/trip labels; fit preprocessing within training folds; grouped chronological holdout and event-level alarms; preserve genuine anomalies.

Recommended future model: CatBoost/ExtraTrees benchmark with independently labeled future events, including raw-feature ablation.

Review status: Pending

Source caveats: 87390 points at 30 s span~30.34 days of samples, not 105 continuous days; coverage/gaps unclear. Cleaned motor temperature minimum 95°C conflicts with stable<85°C rule. Data availability statement says no datasets analyzed despite empirical study. Do not advertise 99.85% field failure prediction.
