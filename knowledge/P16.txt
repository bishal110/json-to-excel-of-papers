Knowledge record: P16-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Two random forests with engineered physics/statistical features

Inputs: 35 raw channels reduced to 18; static completion/install/failure records; means, differences, SD and calibrated virtual rates/efficiencies. Three years used because physics-derived history limited.

Outputs: Binary well failure next 30 days; pump failure next 45 days.

Reported performance: Initial well test: TP55, FN42, TN96, FP1; precision 98.2%, recall 56.7%, accuracy 77.8%. Pump test: TP6, FN4, TN21, FP1; precision 85.7%, recall 60%, accuracy 84.4%. Blind 35-well review: well TP17, FN12, TN27, FP2 => precision 89.47%, recall 58.62%, accuracy 75.86%. Pump TP12, FN6, TN28, FP1 => precision 92.31%, recall 66.67%, calculated accuracy 85.11%; paper prints 88.11% and 39 correct although matrix has 40 correct (p.20).

Advantages: Physics features and separate component target; blind wells; high positive predictive value in study.

Limitations: Small component sample, SME-corrected event dates, balanced initial test prevalence and retrospective review. Roughly 40% well failures missed. Complete 18-feature names not disclosed.

Data feasibility: Not directly from three stated sources alone: requires completion/equipment/failure records and validated physical models; missing downhole sensors remove important inputs. Reduced-signal model requires separate training.

Complexity: High

Prediction target: Aggregate failure risk and pump-component risk.

Lead time qualification: Nominal 30/45 day labels; historical blind averages about 60 days are a different measurement, with broad ranges including 0 days for well failures.

Signals: Measured telemetry, physical model outputs, maintenance/install outcomes.

Source thresholds: No operational risk cutoff disclosed. >75% data quality over 120 days is a training gate.

Implementation proposal: Reconstruct independent ESP-run timelines, causal features and physical models; prospective shadow alerts; measure event precision/recall, false alarms per well-month and lead distributions.

Recommended future model: Hybrid physics plus tree model, separately validated for full-sensor and surface-only cohorts.

Review status: Pending

Source caveats: Figure 21 matrix visually verified. Printed blind pump accuracy does not reconcile. Estimated benefits are not field-achieved savings; authors' broad claims about uncontrollable component failures are not universal engineering rules.
