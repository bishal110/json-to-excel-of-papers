Knowledge record: P21-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: EM Gaussian-mixture labeling plus physical-rule filtering; multiclass RBF SVM.

Inputs: 1947 rod pumps, five fields; train 2010, validate 2011; four primary trends plus card unchanged days/runtime ratio.

Outputs: Normal, pre-failure and failure classes for tubing/pump.

Reported performance: TP278, FP145, FN141, TN1383 =>precision 65.7%, recall 66.4%. Field precision 50.0–80.2%, recall 47.1–88.5%. Historical evaluation; deployment future work.

Advantages: Cross-field training and chronological holdout; documents sudden failures cannot be anticipated from absent trends.

Limitations: 100 day matching tolerance; alerts within 15 days of downtime filtered; different fields vary markedly; prose incorrectly labels false positive as false negative; normal-cluster cutoff 70% prose versus 50% algorithm.

Data feasibility: Rod-card variables absent in ESP sources; methodology transferable only after new ESP training/labels.

Complexity: High

Prediction target: Trend-bearing rod pump/tubing failures; abrupt rod/joint events not reliably forecast.

Lead time qualification: 100 day evaluation tolerance is not proven 100 day advance warning; actual failure may precede recorded discovery.

Signals: Load-card/runtime series and independent event dates.

Source thresholds: 100 nonmissing signatures; normal cluster>50% algorithm versus 70% prose; tubing-card shrinkage lambda unspecified.

Implementation proposal: Use source methodology to design cross-well ESP study; audit true onset vs discovery date and report abrupt-event detection separately.

Recommended future model: Global ESP classifier with well/time holdout and cohort-specific performance.

Review status: Pending

Source caveats: Source data are rod pumps. Do not transfer rod-cutting/card rules directly to ESP. Training ratio statement is inconsistent with stated 50 failure/350 normal well count.
