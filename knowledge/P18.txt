Knowledge record: P18-D01
Type: Rules

Rule name: Health anomaly

Source status: candidate_requires_local_validation

IF: T-squared exceeds healthy 95% confidence limit

AND: Baseline and current operating state comparable and data quality valid

THEN: Flag anomalous ESP operation, not a proven component cause.

Engineering explanation: PCA reduces correlated signals; Mahalanobis-type distance measures departure.

Alternative causes: Other source-specific conditions plus operating changes and data errors; no quantified comparative likelihood.

Recommended checks: Verify premises, units, operating state and data freshness. PCA reduces correlated signals; Mahalanobis-type distance measures departure.

Knowledge record: P18-I01
Type: Indicators

Indicator: Hotelling T-squared excursion

Physical meaning: Correlated deviation from healthy operation.

Required parameters: Standardized measured variables and robust baseline covariance.

Typical trend: Increases beyond 95% confidence bound; case exceeds baseline by orders of magnitude.

Lead time qualification: Example deviation before failure at minute 726; second event corrective 580–690 min, new problem~1100, trip 1518. No general lead distribution.

Knowledge record: P18-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Robust PCA and Hotelling T-squared statistical monitoring

Inputs: Two years of minute data, 22 variables; robust treatment of roughly 25% outliers.

Outputs: Health KPI and abnormal operating-state alerts.

Reported performance: Six PCs explain>96% variance; two explain about two-thirds. These are variance-retention figures, not detection accuracy. No sensitivity/specificity/event denominator.

Advantages: Can learn normal behavior without labeled failures; compact dashboard.

Limitations: Limited illustrated cases; no validated causal diagnosis; 95% pointwise bound does not equal 5% fleet event false-alarm rate; operating regime shifts matter.

Data feasibility: Potential with mapped Avalon channels; full reproduction requires 22 inputs, whose complete names are not disclosed. Missing-sensor cohort must use its own baseline. Excel tests are context, not minute inputs.

Complexity: Medium

Prediction target: Departure from normal multivariate operation.

Lead time qualification: Case-specific hours; no population warning distribution or RUL.

Signals: Consistent synchronized multichannel telemetry.

Source thresholds: 95% confidence bound on T-squared; model-specific, not physical trip.

Implementation proposal: Fit healthy regime-specific baselines; exclude startup/shutdown transients as appropriate; require persistence and contribution review; evaluate false alerts and event detection.

Recommended future model: Robust PCA baseline as interpretable comparator to more complex models.

Review status: Pending

Source caveats: Related to P05; do not import P05's 30 hour example into this source. Contribution-based diagnosis/prescription described as future extension.
