Knowledge record: P23-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: DNN(36 time/frequency features)+two CNN branches(STFT, CWT128×128 RGB) fused to 5 class softmax.

Inputs: Manufacturer laboratory water tests; 36 accelerometers over 6 components; 4096 Hz.9690 stated samples.

Outputs: Normal, unbalance, misalignment, rubbing, sensor fault.

Reported performance: Reported mean accuracy~99.98%; Table 6 recall 100% exceptmisalignment 99.93%; AUC100% reported. Class counts 3774+2703+1938+969+255=9639, not 9690; split 7752/1938 matches 9690. Repetition count 10 versus 25 intext.

Advantages: Combines complementary waveform representations and explicitly distinguishesensor fault.

Limitations: Labwater data; random record split may share machine/test run signatures; no held-out field run validation or predictive lead; high training cost.

Data feasibility: No, unless Avalon contains raw 4096 Hz waveforms with correct positions. HF operating telemetry is not automatically vibration waveform data. Excel sources cannot substitute.

Complexity: High

Prediction target: Present mechanical/sensor fault class; future failure not validated.

Lead time qualification: Not reported.

Signals: Raw accelerometers and speed.

Source thresholds: No physical alarm cutoffs; spectral roll off.85 is feature definition.

Implementation proposal: Confirm waveform availability; first run simple spectral/baseline classifiers; split by physical unit/test run before investing in deep fusion.

Recommended future model: Laboratory/bench QA classifier or field waveform classifier after data acquisition.

Review status: Pending

Source caveats: Table 6 visually verified. Near-perfect classification is not field Pd M accuracy. Dataset counts/repeat protocol internally inconsistent.
