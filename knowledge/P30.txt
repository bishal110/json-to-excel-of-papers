Knowledge record: P30-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Bayesian/HMM-like piecewise signal segmentation (top 200 paths), trend/noise/correlation probabilities, expert Bayesian network with manually/equation-set CPTs.

Inputs: Minute telemetry and physical/reference knowledge.

Outputs: Probabilities of stress patterns and failures.

Reported performance: 57 field/offline events: 37 low flow, 12 deadhead, 6 gas, 2 mechanical; 14 missed by engineer, 10≥1 h earlier, 1 wrong diagnosis later addressed, 32 same-time. Additional 22 proof-of-concept=>79 total; no independent healthy-time denominator or fleet false alarm rate.

Advantages: Interpretable uncertainty and explicitly limited missing-input handling.

Limitations: Reference tuning after frequency change;~5% unusual wells require tuning; incomplete sensors+unusual conditions degrade accuracy; 57 event selection does not prove universal 100% recall.

Data feasibility: Some partial-sensor cases supported, 17/79 events with≥1 missing parameter. Loss ofboth PIP/PDP violates source crucial-input gate; surface-only cannot claim this method's diagnostic performance.

Complexity: High

Prediction target: Stress-pattern detection before possible damage; existing fault diagnosis.

Lead time qualification: 10/57 events≥1 h before engineer report; stress-to-failure time not calibrated.

Signals: Eight core inputs with quality/reference context.

Source thresholds: ~75% event probability; trend threshold examples~40% strong change,~5% wear; sensor-resolution-specific noise limits.

Implementation proposal: Implement explainable candidate logic first; calibrate priors locally, abstain on missing critical evidence; measure alarm workload and confirmed outcomes.

Recommended future model: Bayesian diagnostic ranking and causal change-point feature extractor.

Review status: Pending
