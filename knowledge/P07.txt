Knowledge record: P07-M01
Type: AI Models

Model type: Ada BNet (Ada Boost+Bayesian networks) and Ada DT (Ada Boost+decision trees)

Inputs: 14+ daily rod-pump attributes with expert preprocessing and normalization

Outputs: Normal, tubing failure, rod failure and pre-signal classes

Reported performance: Narrative reports Ada BNet normal 97.7%, failure 91.9%; Table 3 has transposed/inconsistent labels/count orientation. Ada DT ~95% best at 80–180 iterations. Training-table 'percent' entries are actually fractional-looking errors; do not reproduce as <0.12% without correction.

Advantages: Single field model rather than one per well; well-level evaluation.

Limitations: Rod systems; recorded-date bias; missing-data fill; table inconsistencies; claimed proof of no overfitting contradicted by reported overfit above 180 iterations; record-level CV may leak within wells.

Data feasibility: No direct ESP implementation with Avalon/tests/pressures: rod card/load inputs missing. General workflow transferable.

Complexity: Medium

Prediction target: Rod-system failure states and pre-signals, not validated ESP failures.

Lead time qualification: 2/8-month examples reference recorded dates, not verified physical onset.

Signals: Rod load/card/production/runtime and adjudicated event labels.

Source thresholds: Learned model votes; no transferable engineering limits.

Implementation proposal: Transfer evaluation/label discipline only; establish ESP-specific features, whole-run temporal holdout and alert-event matching.

Recommended future model: Boosted classifier benchmark after complete ESP retraining.

Review status: Pending

Source caveats: Normal is a class, not a failure mode. No injection analysis.

Source report: reports/P07.md. PDF page references refer to the original paper, not this text file.
