Knowledge record: P29-A02
Type: Actions

Condition: Harsh service/tight-hole damage

Immediate actions: Review fluid chemistry, clearance and damage location.

Short term actions: Select compatible coatings/elastomers/metallurgy and cable protection with supplier.

Long term actions: Source upgrades: coated pump/intake/discharge, Ni-Cu shafts/housings, lead-sheath cable, tandem seals, modified low-profile heads/grooves/centralizers.

Prevention: Maintain field-specific specifications and specialized spares; assess full-cable replacement rather than routine new-to-old splicing.

Expected benefit: Less corrosion, scale adhesion and installation damage.

Type: AI Models

Model type: RCFA and descriptive reliability trending; no ML.

Inputs: Operating/design/material/DIFA/history records.

Outputs: Evidence-supported root cause and preventive action.

Reported performance: No diagnostic accuracy. MTBF chart 196,190,168,292,254,628 days for 1995–2000; active runs require censoring in formal reliability analysis.

Advantages: Directly actionable evidence and prevention workflow.

Limitations: Small field, concurrent interventions, ambiguous improvement percentages/economic units.

Data feasibility: Avalon+Excel support surveillance but not full RCFA; needs DIFA, materials, chemistry, settings, installation records.

Complexity: Low for workflow; Medium for integrated reliability analysis

Signals: Telemetry plus design and forensic records.
