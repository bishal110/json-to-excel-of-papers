## SECTION 8: MACHINE LEARNING / AI CONTENT


**Model Type:** Linear regression, decision tree and logistic regression at multiple time scales.

**Inputs:** Geology/trajectory plus whole-run or current-period summaries; 42 paired failure/non failure periods; all-period set 29 fail/891 nonfail.

**Outputs:** Total life, active hours-to-failure and period failure probability.

**Accuracy:** Geology+geometry R².41(adj.38); hours-on regression R².75(adj.73), tree.82. Paired classifier TP30, FN12, FP18, TN24=>recall 71.43%, precision 62.5%. All-period fitted TP25, FN4, FP1, TN890=>recall 86.21%, precision 96.15%, but evaluated on fitting data; authors acknowledge optimism and unsuccessful small-data validation.

**Advantages:** Distinguishes calendar time, active exposure and failure versus other stops.

**Limitations:** Completed-run summaries leak future information if used as prospective predictors; only 29/42 failure cases; correlated features; no reliable external validation or forecast lead.

**Can it be implemented using only Avalon Data / Production Test Data / Surface Pressure Data?:** Surface concepts transferable, but SAGD steam/injector/geological inputs absent from stated sources. Ordinary water injection cannot substitute for SAGD steam variables.

**Complexity:** Medium
