## SECTION 2: OPERATIONAL PROBLEM


**What practical field problem is being solved?:** Single-variable reactive monitoring misses joint operating changes and offers little guidance on causes or corrections.

**What production losses are associated with this problem?:** Illustrative assumptions: 850 b/d liquid, 35% water cut, 30 downtime days/year, $60/bbl yield $994,500 deferred oil value/year. Not measured savings. Source intervention examples: $5–25 k conventional onshore, $150–250 k unconventional, up to $1 m offshore (p. 3).

**How would an offshore production engineer recognize this problem?:** PCA scores leave healthy cluster; T² grows markedly; contribution chart prioritizes suspect variables (pp. 9–13).


## SECTION 3: FAILURE MODES IDENTIFIED


### P05-F01: Motor overheating from underutilization

**Failure Mode:** Motor overheating from underutilization

**Category:** Motor

**Symptoms:** Rising motor temperature and departing PCA scores

**Root Causes:** Low flow/underutilization in documented sequence

**Consequences:** Trip risk

**Typical Field Indicators:** Motor temperature highest contribution

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** pp. 10–12

### P05-F02: Gas interference after excessive drawdown

**Failure Mode:** Gas interference after excessive drawdown

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Initial improvement followed by renewed instability

**Root Causes:** Choke adjustment lowers wellbore pressure below bubble point

**Consequences:** Gas production and eventual trip

**Typical Field Indicators:** Pressure/gas context and PCA trajectory

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** p. 10

## SECTION 8: MACHINE LEARNING / AI CONTENT


**Model Type:** Robust PCA (configured for 25% outliers), T² surveillance, contribution diagnostics, quadratic conditional operating bands

**Inputs:** 22 measured/derived variables; one-minute historian plus event records

**Outputs:** Health trajectory, scalar anomaly score, ranked contributions and suggested stable bands

**Accuracy:** >96% explained variance is not detection accuracy. All studied events reportedly detected hours early; event denominator/false-positive rate and independent prospective test not supplied.

**Advantages:** Interpretable visual trajectory and contributors; relatively modest mathematical complexity.

**Limitations:** Linear normal model; correlated data; anonymized inputs; fixed-other-variable bands are not causal control policy; no held-out performance counts.

**Can it be implemented using only Avalon Data / Production Test Data / Surface Pressure Data?:** Full replication requires downhole/thermal/vibration/leakage data and unnamed tags. A reduced Avalon+surface-pressure model is feasible as a new validated model; sparse production tests add context, not minute-scale signals.

**Complexity:** Medium
