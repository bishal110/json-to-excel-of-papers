## SECTION 3: FAILURE MODES IDENTIFIED


### P26-F01: Gas interference

**Failure Mode:** Gas interference

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Reduced developed load/delivery.

**Root Causes:** Excess free gas.

**Consequences:** Production loss/damage.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Hydraulic and electrical changes; no tested class rule.

**Confidence Level:** Low/Medium

**Evidence:** pp.2–3

### P26-F02: Viscous-fluid performance loss

**Failure Mode:** Viscous-fluid performance loss

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Efficiency reduction compared with water.

**Root Causes:** Higher viscosity/non-Newtonian service.

**Consequences:** Reduced capacity and altered duty.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Pressure/rate/speed and fluid characterization.

**Confidence Level:** Medium for context; unvalidated in model

**Evidence:** pp.4–6

### P26-F03: Aggregate instantaneous/partial failure

**Failure Mode:** Aggregate instantaneous/partial failure

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Loss/reduction of lift.

**Root Causes:** Not isolated; pressure-build-up assumption incomplete.

**Consequences:** Deferred production.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Pressure prediction departure; no valid universal direction.

**Confidence Level:** Low

**Evidence:** pp.9–11
