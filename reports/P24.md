## SECTION 3: FAILURE MODES IDENTIFIED


### P24-F01: Cable insulation/electrical failure

**Failure Mode:** Cable insulation/electrical failure

**Category:** Cable

**Symptoms:** Electrical downhole failure; specific continuous signature not isolated.

**Root Causes:** Corrosion, material failure, abrasion, overload.

**Consequences:** Workover and deferment.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Combined telemetry and electrical testing.

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** pp.2–3

### P24-F02: Motor stator overheating

**Failure Mode:** Motor stator overheating

**Category:** Motor

**Symptoms:** Motor temperature rises; frequency rampsdown at thermal limit.

**Root Causes:** Polymer deposition reduces heat dissipation, raising T and promoting further deposition.

**Consequences:** Stator failure and shutdown.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Motor temperature/current/frequency and polymer context.

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** p.3

### P24-F03: Polymer intake plugging

**Failure Mode:** Polymer intake plugging

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Flow restriction; hot motor region.

**Root Causes:** Thermallypromoted polymer precipitation.

**Consequences:** Loss of cooling/delivery.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Thermal/hydraulic response; not separately classified.

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** p.3

### P24-F04: Downhole sensor electrical failure

**Failure Mode:** Downhole sensor electrical failure

**Category:** Sensor

**Symptoms:** Electrical failure category include sensor.

**Root Causes:** Electrical component fault; no unique mechanism specified.

**Consequences:** Loss of monitoring.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Missing/invalid readings and electrical checks.

**Confidence Level:** Low

**Evidence:** p.3

### P24-F05: Tubing leak

**Failure Mode:** Tubing leak

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Discharge pressure↓; PIP/annulus pressure↑ in explanatory example.

**Root Causes:** Tubing integrity loss.

**Consequences:** Recirculation and production loss.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Correlated pressure departure.

**Confidence Level:** Low/Medium; illustrative not validated case

**Evidence:** p.5

## SECTION 8: MACHINE LEARNING / AI CONTENT


**Model Type:** PCA+XGBoost; hourly median, 1.5 IQR filter, standardization, moving differences.

**Inputs:** 339089 normal, 1728 preworkover, 288 workover hourly points; 8 physical signals+differences.

**Outputs:** Normal versus≤7 day pre-event; discussion uses normal/preworkover/workover classes.

**Accuracy:** 10 fold mean ROC-AUC.95; test pre-event precision.80, recall.60, F1.71, support 604; normal 101726. Abstract says>0.71, table.71.

**Advantages:** Clear physical polymer mechanism and explicit imbalanced-class metrics.

**Limitations:** 70/30 versus 70/15/15 split descriptions; random folds not well/run chronological validation; full-data PCA/scaling language risks leakage; tables contain impossible descriptive ordering(mean/std versus max); standardization text says variance rather than SD.

**Can it be implemented using only Avalon Data / Production Test Data / Surface Pressure Data?:** Full model needs downhole sensors and workover labels. Three sources alone insufficient unless labels/equipment context supplied. Sensorless variant untested.

**Complexity:** Medium
