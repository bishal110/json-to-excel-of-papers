## SECTION 2: OPERATIONAL PROBLEM


**What practical field problem is being solved?:** Unrecognized restrictions, gas interference and integrity problems persist despite abundant telemetry.

**What production losses are associated with this problem?:** Reduced flow, trips, heating, wear and repair downtime. Five cases demonstrate feasibility; no controlled aggregate oil gain or avoided-failure rate.

**How would an offshore production engineer recognize this problem?:** Match simultaneous trends at comparable frequency; pressure location differentiates surface valve, tubing restriction and pump restriction. Similar pump patterns remain ambiguous.
