## SECTION 2: OPERATIONAL PROBLEM


**What practical field problem is being solved?:** Repeated restarts and incomplete evidence obscure the actual cause; harsh sour/scaling service and tight-hole installation damage shorten life.

**What production losses are associated with this problem?:** Reported average run life~200 days to>600 days; servicing cost per intervention increased~50%, overall expenditure declined. Earlier losses 12 days/year/well and 3000 bbl; claimed decrease '2000 bbl/day' is dimensionally inconsistent and should not be used as a measured daily saving (p.4).

**How would an offshore production engineer recognize this problem?:** Recurring failure by well/installation crew/material, changed trip settings, scale/thrust damage, ground faults, poor power and incompatible chemicals.

## SECTION 7: RECOMMENDED ACTIONS


### Failure/recurrence

**Immediate Actions:** Preserve drive history, charts and physical evidence; qualified diagnosis before restart.

**Short-Term Actions:** Joint operator/vendor DIFA; separate observations from causal interpretation; compare previous pulls and startup settings.

**Long-Term Actions:** Implement confirmed material, design, power or operating corrections and verify outcomes.

**Preventive Measures:** Author recommends operating/test review at least biweekly and quarterly optimization; local risk may require more frequent review.

**Expected Benefits:** Longer life and fewer repeat failures.

**Expected Production Impact:** ~200 to>600 day field association; not controlled proof of a single measure.

**Attribution:** pp.1–5

### Harsh service/tight-hole damage

**Immediate Actions:** Review fluid chemistry, clearance and damage location.

**Short-Term Actions:** Select compatible coatings/elastomers/metallurgy and cable protection with supplier.

**Long-Term Actions:** Source upgrades: coated pump/intake/discharge, Ni-Cu shafts/housings, lead-sheath cable, tandem seals, modified low-profile heads/grooves/centralizers.

**Preventive Measures:** Maintain field-specific specifications and specialized spares; assess full-cable replacement rather than routine new-to-old splicing.

**Expected Benefits:** Less corrosion, scale adhesion and installation damage.

**Expected Production Impact:** Servicing cost increased~50% but total expenditure reported lower.

**Attribution:** pp.3–4; historical field-specific specification, not current universal material standard.
