## SECTION 3: FAILURE MODES IDENTIFIED


### P21-F01: Rod pump failure

**Failure Mode:** Rod pump failure

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Abnormal dynamometer/load/runtime trends; sometimes abrupt.

**Root Causes:** Wear/design/manufacturing, solids/gas and corrosive fluids; no unique model cause.

**Consequences:** Production loss and workover.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Rod-pump-specific load-card features.

**Confidence Level:** Medium for rod pumps; Low transfer to ESP

**Evidence:** Methods and field cases

### P21-F02: Tubing leak

**Failure Mode:** Tubing leak

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Loss of useful production while pump may keep operating.

**Root Causes:** Mechanical wear/rod cutting and corrosion.

**Consequences:** Recirculation and sustained production loss.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Dynamometer card shrinkage plus production evidence.

**Confidence Level:** Medium for rod pumps

**Evidence:** Problem description

## SECTION 8: MACHINE LEARNING / AI CONTENT


**Model Type:** EM Gaussian-mixture labeling plus physical-rule filtering; multiclass RBF SVM.

**Inputs:** 1947 rod pumps, five fields; train 2010, validate 2011; four primary trends plus card unchanged days/runtime ratio.

**Outputs:** Normal, pre-failure and failure classes for tubing/pump.

**Accuracy:** TP278, FP145, FN141, TN1383 =>precision 65.7%, recall 66.4%. Field precision 50.0–80.2%, recall 47.1–88.5%. Historical evaluation; deployment future work.

**Advantages:** Cross-field training and chronological holdout; documents sudden failures cannot be anticipated from absent trends.

**Limitations:** 100 day matching tolerance; alerts within 15 days of downtime filtered; different fields vary markedly; prose incorrectly labels false positive as false negative; normal-cluster cutoff 70% prose versus 50% algorithm.

**Can it be implemented using only Avalon Data / Production Test Data / Surface Pressure Data?:** Rod-card variables absent in ESP sources; methodology transferable only after new ESP training/labels.

**Complexity:** High
