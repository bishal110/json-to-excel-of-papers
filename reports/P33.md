## SECTION 2: OPERATIONAL PROBLEM


**What practical field problem is being solved?:** Sparse, poorly labeled faults and geological variation limit data-only classifiers.

**What production losses are associated with this problem?:** Unplanned shutdown, reduced production and workover expense; no measured production uplift established.

**How would an offshore production engineer recognize this problem?:** Surface current/voltage, wellhead pressures/temperature and estimated rates show increases, decreases, fluctuations or zero drops.
