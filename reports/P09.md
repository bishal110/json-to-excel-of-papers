## SECTION 1: PAPER METADATA


**Title:** Condition monitoring and fault diagnosis of electric submersible pump based on wellhead electrical parameters and production parameters

**Authors:** Lu Li; Chenquan Hua; Xuan Xu

**Publication Year:** 2018

**Journal/Conference:** Systems Science & Control Engineering, 6(3), 253–261

**DOI (if available):** 10.1080/21642583.2018.1548983

**Primary Objective:** Diagnose ESP operating conditions with surface electrical and production signals to reduce reliance on downhole instruments.


## SECTION 2: OPERATIONAL PROBLEM


**What practical field problem is being solved?:** Downhole sensor exposure/cost and ambiguity of single-current diagnostics.

**What production losses are associated with this problem?:** Impaired lift, protective shutdown and lost production; no quantified savings.

**How would an offshore production engineer recognize this problem?:** Combine normalized current mean/variance/waveform with voltage, wellhead pressure, liquid/gas rate and gas–liquid ratio (PDF pp. 3–9).


## SECTION 3: FAILURE MODES IDENTIFIED


### P09-F01: Underload

**Failure Mode:** Underload

**Category:** Motor

**Symptoms:** Current decreases toward threshold

**Root Causes:** Low load; cause not uniquely determined

**Consequences:** Protective shutdown

**Typical Field Indicators:** Normalized current and liquid response

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** PDF pp. 4–5, 8

### P09-F02: Overload

**Failure Mode:** Overload

**Category:** Motor

**Symptoms:** Current rises toward trip level; liquid decreases in schematic

**Root Causes:** Excess load

**Consequences:** Trip/production loss

**Typical Field Indicators:** Current above baseline, GLR stable

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** PDF pp. 4–6

### P09-F03: Gas interference

**Failure Mode:** Gas interference

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Current variance and GLR rise

**Root Causes:** Free gas changes pump loading

**Consequences:** Reduced stable hydraulic capacity

**Typical Field Indicators:** Current fluctuation, gas/liquid rates

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** PDF pp. 4, 6

### P09-F04: Gas locking

**Failure Mode:** Gas locking

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Large current fluctuations then underload shutdown; liquid declines

**Root Causes:** Excess gas in pump

**Consequences:** Loss of lift

**Typical Field Indicators:** GLR rises, fluctuating decreasing current

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** PDF pp. 4, 6

### P09-F05: Insufficient liquid supply

**Failure Mode:** Insufficient liquid supply

**Category:** Reservoir

**Symptoms:** Current decreases, cycles off/restarts; liquid falls

**Root Causes:** Moving fluid level falls relative to intake

**Consequences:** Underload shutdown

**Typical Field Indicators:** Current slope, GLR, liquid supply

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** PDF p. 6

### P09-F06: Tubing/tool-string leak

**Failure Mode:** Tubing/tool-string leak

**Category:** Surface Network

**Symptoms:** Pressure and delivered liquid fall, current approximately unchanged

**Root Causes:** Tubing-to-annulus leakage

**Consequences:** Lost delivered production

**Typical Field Indicators:** Wellhead pressure/rate with current

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** PDF pp. 6–7; component downhole tubing

### P09-F07: Overvoltage

**Failure Mode:** Overvoltage

**Category:** VSD

**Symptoms:** Voltage/current/production rise then trip; GLR stable

**Root Causes:** Supply above rated value

**Consequences:** Protective shutdown

**Typical Field Indicators:** Voltage versus rating

**Confidence Level:** Low

**Evidence:** PDF pp. 7–8

### P09-F08: Undervoltage

**Failure Mode:** Undervoltage

**Category:** VSD

**Symptoms:** Source schematic shows voltage/current/rates decreasing then trip

**Root Causes:** Supply below rating

**Consequences:** Reduced lifting / shutdown

**Typical Field Indicators:** Voltage and hydraulic response

**Confidence Level:** Low

**Evidence:** PDF pp. 7–8

### P09-F09: Impeller eccentric wear / sand plugging; bearing eccentric wear; excessive shaft thrust

**Failure Mode:** Impeller eccentric wear / sand plugging; bearing eccentric wear; excessive shaft thrust

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Discussed only in cited background

**Root Causes:** Mechanical wear/solids

**Consequences:** Mechanical degradation

**Typical Field Indicators:** Vibration cited, not validated by this surface scheme

**Confidence Level:** Low

**Evidence:** PDF p. 2
