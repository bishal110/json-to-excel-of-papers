## SECTION 4: EARLY WARNING INDICATORS


### P18-I01: Hotelling T-squared excursion

**Indicator Name:** Hotelling T-squared excursion

**Physical Meaning:** Correlated deviation from healthy operation.

**Parameter Required:** Standardized measured variables and robust baseline covariance.

**Typical Trend:** Increases beyond 95% confidence bound; case exceeds baseline by orders of magnitude.

**How long before failure may this indicator appear?:** Example deviation before failure at minute 726; second event corrective 580–690 min, new problem~1100, trip 1518. No general lead distribution.

## SECTION 8: MACHINE LEARNING / AI CONTENT


**Model Type:** Robust PCA and Hotelling T-squared statistical monitoring

**Inputs:** Two years of minute data, 22 variables; robust treatment of roughly 25% outliers.

**Outputs:** Health KPI and abnormal operating-state alerts.

**Accuracy:** Six PCs explain>96% variance; two explain about two-thirds. These are variance-retention figures, not detection accuracy. No sensitivity/specificity/event denominator.

**Advantages:** Can learn normal behavior without labeled failures; compact dashboard.

**Limitations:** Limited illustrated cases; no validated causal diagnosis; 95% pointwise bound does not equal 5% fleet event false-alarm rate; operating regime shifts matter.

**Can it be implemented using only Avalon Data / Production Test Data / Surface Pressure Data?:** Potential with mapped Avalon channels; full reproduction requires 22 inputs, whose complete names are not disclosed. Missing-sensor cohort must use its own baseline. Excel tests are context, not minute inputs.

**Complexity:** Medium
