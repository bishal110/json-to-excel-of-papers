**What practical field problem is being solved?:** Marginal field with>20 installations and 13 failures; electrical faults dominate and simple trends can obscure drift.

**What production losses are associated with this problem?:** After ground-fault failures, gas-lift fallback delivered~75% of previous ESP performance in Well A and 50% in Well B; constrained by gas availability. No demonstrated model-caused uplift.

**How would an offshore production engineer recognize this problem?:** Lost/frozen downhole displays, ground-fault electrical integrity tests, stall trips and same-frequency multivariate drift.

## SECTION 8: MACHINE LEARNING / AI CONTENT


**Model Type:** Standardized PCA2 D plus Azure two-class boosted decision tree screenshot.

**Inputs:** 8 variables abstract versus 10 Dmethod; 3 failed+1 operating case; tree 70/30 split.

**Outputs:** Normal/failed status and PCA drift.

**Accuracy:** PCA PC1=78.47%, PC2=9.94%, total 88.41% variance, not accuracy. Screenshot positive class NORMAL: TP441, FN0, FP14, TN56; accuracy.973, precision.969, recall 1, F1.984, AUC.927. Recast failure-positive: TP56, FN14, FP0, TN441 =>failure recall 80%, precision 100% in reported sample.

**Advantages:** Direct sensor-loss/ground-fault examples and frequency-conditioning lesson.

**Limitations:** Small cases; automatic real-time deployment future work; tree labels/sample independence unspecified; months-long retrospective drift can reflect onset/discovery ambiguity.

**Can it be implemented using only Avalon Data / Production Test Data / Surface Pressure Data?:** Full PCA unavailable after downhole loss; build separate surface baseline. Electrical integrity/DIFA/metadata are additional required records.

**Complexity:** Medium
