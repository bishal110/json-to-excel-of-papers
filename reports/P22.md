## SECTION 2: OPERATIONAL PROBLEM


**What practical field problem is being solved?:** Manual visits/amp charts miss developing off-design operation and delayed optimization.

**What production losses are associated with this problem?:** Claims production restoration beyond 2017 plan and current 4.6 year run life compared with cited 3 year industry average; no controlled attribution or numeric production uplift (pp.9–11).

**How would an offshore production engineer recognize this problem?:** Compare normalized motor loading and inferred rate/head to actual speed-dependent pump curve; check intake pressure, temperature and surface pressure drop.


## SECTION 3: FAILURE MODES IDENTIFIED


### P22-F01: Upthrust

**Failure Mode:** Upthrust

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Flow above recommended maximum at operating speed.

**Root Causes:** Excess pump throughput relative to curve.

**Consequences:** Thrust wear and reduced life.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Rate/curve and differential pressure at known speed/density.

**Confidence Level:** High for operating-envelope concept

**Evidence:** pp.4, 10

### P22-F02: Downthrust

**Failure Mode:** Downthrust

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Flow below recommended minimum.

**Root Causes:** Excess restriction or insufficient inflow.

**Consequences:** Thrust wear and heating.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Rate/curve and differential pressure.

**Confidence Level:** High for concept

**Evidence:** pp.4, 10

### P22-F03: Pump-off / excessive drawdown

**Failure Mode:** Pump-off / excessive drawdown

**Category:** Reservoir

**Symptoms:** Low PIP or fluid level.

**Root Causes:** Inflow insufficient for pumping demand.

**Consequences:** Gas/cooling issues and trips.

**Typical Field Indicators:** PIP versus approved minimum/bubblepoint context.

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** pp.3–4

### P22-F04: Deadhead/low-flow operation

**Failure Mode:** Deadhead/low-flow operation

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Abnormal PDP and low flow.

**Root Causes:** High downstream restriction.

**Consequences:** Heating and pump damage.

**Typical Field Indicators:** PDP, rate, choke, wellhead pressure.

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** p.4

### P22-F05: Motor overheating/burnout

**Failure Mode:** Motor overheating/burnout

**Category:** Motor

**Symptoms:** Motor winding temperature rise.

**Root Causes:** Inadequate cooling/excess duty.

**Consequences:** Motor loss.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Motor temperature responds before intake temperature in source discussion.

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** p.4

### P22-F06: Underload/overload trip

**Failure Mode:** Underload/overload trip

**Category:** Motor

**Symptoms:** Current outside configured operating range.

**Root Causes:** Low rate, density change, oversized motor or excess loading.

**Consequences:** Production interruptions.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Current/nameplate ratio and surface pressure differential.

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** pp.8–10

### P22-F07: Y-tool bypass recirculation

**Failure Mode:** Y-tool bypass recirculation

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Potential discharge return to wellbore.

**Root Causes:** Blanking plug not in place during normal operation.

**Consequences:** Lost effective delivery.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Completion/intervention state; no validated trend supplied.

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** p.2

## SECTION 7: RECOMMENDED ACTIONS


### Off-design duty

**Immediate Actions:** Verify curve, speed, density and ratings.

**Short-Term Actions:** Engineer choke/frequency optimization within well and network constraints.

**Long-Term Actions:** Resize/upgrade/downgrade ESP when duty mismatch persistent.

**Preventive Measures:** Maintain equipment curves, PI and installation/operating best practices.

**Expected Benefits:** Stable production and reduced thrust stress.

**Expected Production Impact:** Source qualitative; no isolated causal gain.

**Attribution:** pp.6–11

### Motor loading mismatch

**Immediate Actions:** Confirm motor/transformer ratings and actual electrical state.

**Short-Term Actions:** Specialist voltage-optimization and motor rerating assessment.

**Long-Term Actions:** Review motor sizing and electrical design.

**Preventive Measures:** Track nameplate-normalized loading and trip history.

**Expected Benefits:** Avoid under/overload trips.

**Expected Production Impact:** Reported loading adjustment 60% to 80%; not universally optimal.

**Attribution:** pp.9–10; do not directly lower voltage based solely on this report.

Timing categories organize the extracted recommendations. Where the author does not prescribe timing, the immediate/short/long ordering is an engineering proposal. No chemical dose, trip bypass, restart sequence or autonomous setpoint is inferred.
