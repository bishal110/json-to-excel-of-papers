**What practical field problem is being solved?:** 150 wells generate>2000 alerts/day, only 5–10 requiring follow-up; alarm overload delays diagnosis.

## SECTION 8: MACHINE LEARNING / AI CONTENT


**Model Type:** Bayesian/HMM-like piecewise signal segmentation (top 200 paths), trend/noise/correlation probabilities, expert Bayesian network with manually/equation-set CPTs.

**Inputs:** Minute telemetry and physical/reference knowledge.

**Outputs:** Probabilities of stress patterns and failures.

**Accuracy:** 57 field/offline events: 37 low flow, 12 deadhead, 6 gas, 2 mechanical; 14 missed by engineer, 10≥1 h earlier, 1 wrong diagnosis later addressed, 32 same-time. Additional 22 proof-of-concept=>79 total; no independent healthy-time denominator or fleet false alarm rate.

**Advantages:** Interpretable uncertainty and explicitly limited missing-input handling.

**Limitations:** Reference tuning after frequency change;~5% unusual wells require tuning; incomplete sensors+unusual conditions degrade accuracy; 57 event selection does not prove universal 100% recall.

**Can it be implemented using only Avalon Data / Production Test Data / Surface Pressure Data?:** Some partial-sensor cases supported, 17/79 events with≥1 missing parameter. Loss ofboth PIP/PDP violates source crucial-input gate; surface-only cannot claim this method's diagnostic performance.

**Complexity:** High
