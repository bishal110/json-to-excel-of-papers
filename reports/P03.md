## SECTION 2: OPERATIONAL PROBLEM


**What practical field problem is being solved?:** Inconsistent expert-dependent surveillance of many complex ESP systems; inherited rule sets may not discriminate actual failures.

**What production losses are associated with this problem?:** Production loss during ESP downtime stated, not quantified. Nine wells: four operating and five failed; selected from >100 offshore Thailand ESPs.

**How would an offshore production engineer recognize this problem?:** Compare daily-median slopes over 7, 30, 60 days; failed systems triggered more short-term premises, but no reliable causal identification (pp. 7–10).

## SECTION 7: RECOMMENDED ACTIONS

**Immediate Actions:** Review actual trends and event timeline

**Short-Term Actions:** Investigate competing causes; compare hydraulic and electrical evidence

**Long-Term Actions:** Capture confirmed failures and expand rules

## SECTION 8: MACHINE LEARNING / AI CONTENT


**Model Type:** Spreadsheet fuzzy inference; triangular memberships; product AND, OR and Most-of coupling; rank DOF directly without separate defuzzification

**Inputs:** Eight daily-median trend variables; slopes normalized with arctangent

**Outputs:** Scores for nine failure-mode hypotheses and possible generic risk indication

**Accuracy:** No validated accuracy. System did not identify actual root causes in five failures. Many zero premises defeated product AND; Most-of remained low. Table 2 includes example scores, not probabilities.

**Advantages:** Transparent and spreadsheet-compatible; offshore dataset and explicit rule matrix.

**Limitations:** Nine selected wells; actual causes not established; scale/unit-sensitive arctangent; common memberships; trip contamination; three identical rule vectors; 30/60-day gaps.

**Can it be implemented using only Avalon Data / Production Test Data / Surface Pressure Data?:** Avalon+production+surface-pressure data could support full rules only where all eight inputs exist. Lost PIP/PDP/temperature makes published rules incomplete; do not replace missing conditions by true.

**Complexity:** Medium
