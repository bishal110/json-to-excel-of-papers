## SECTION 3: FAILURE MODES IDENTIFIED


### P23-F01: Pump blade unbalance

**Failure Mode:** Pump blade unbalance

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Altered rotational harmonics.

**Root Causes:** Blade/rotating mass imbalance.

**Consequences:** Vibration and potential mechanical damage.

**Typical Field Indicators:** First/second shaft-order harmonic behavior.

**Confidence Level:** Medium for laboratory

**Evidence:** p.9

### P23-F02: Shaft misalignment

**Failure Mode:** Shaft misalignment

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Altered harmonic vibration.

**Root Causes:** Misaligned rotating assembly.

**Consequences:** Mechanical stress.

**Typical Field Indicators:** First/second shaft-order features; not unique frequency diagnosis.

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** p.9

### P23-F03: Mechanical rubbing

**Failure Mode:** Mechanical rubbing

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Low-frequency noise.

**Root Causes:** Contact between components.

**Consequences:** Wear/vibration.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Low-frequency acceleration content.

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** p.9

### P23-F04: Accelerometer fault

**Failure Mode:** Accelerometer fault

**Category:** Sensor

**Symptoms:** Distinct abnormal waveform.

**Root Causes:** Faulty measurement hardware; exact defect not described.

**Consequences:** Misdiagnosis if mistaken for pump fault.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Sensor-class waveform features.

**Confidence Level:** Medium

**Evidence:** pp.9–14

### P23-F05: Mechanical looseness

**Failure Mode:** Mechanical looseness

**Category:** Pump

**Symptoms:** Vibration anomaly in related work.

**Root Causes:** Loosened assembly; not a class tested here.

**Consequences:** Potential mechanical degradation.

**Typical Field Indicators:** Not validated in this study.

**Confidence Level:** Low

**Evidence:** p.2 related work

## SECTION 8: MACHINE LEARNING / AI CONTENT


**Model Type:** DNN(36 time/frequency features)+two CNN branches(STFT, CWT128×128 RGB) fused to 5 class softmax.

**Inputs:** Manufacturer laboratory water tests; 36 accelerometers over 6 components; 4096 Hz.9690 stated samples.

**Outputs:** Normal, unbalance, misalignment, rubbing, sensor fault.

**Accuracy:** Reported mean accuracy~99.98%; Table 6 recall 100% exceptmisalignment 99.93%; AUC100% reported. Class counts 3774+2703+1938+969+255=9639, not 9690; split 7752/1938 matches 9690. Repetition count 10 versus 25 intext.

**Advantages:** Combines complementary waveform representations and explicitly distinguishesensor fault.

**Limitations:** Labwater data; random record split may share machine/test run signatures; no held-out field run validation or predictive lead; high training cost.

**Can it be implemented using only Avalon Data / Production Test Data / Surface Pressure Data?:** No, unless Avalon contains raw 4096 Hz waveforms with correct positions. HF operating telemetry is not automatically vibration waveform data. Excel sources cannot substitute.

**Complexity:** High
